# Cookie & Tracking Disclosure

**Last updated:** 22 March 2026

This disclosure describes how Wuwu Advisor currently uses cookies and similar technologies.  We strive to keep tracking minimal and transparent.  The information below reflects the present implementation; if we introduce new tracking technologies, we will update this disclosure and, where required, seek your consent.

## 1. Essential cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website or app to function and cannot be turned off.  They do not store personally identifiable information.

| Cookie / storage item | Purpose | Expiry |
|---|---|---|
| Session token | Keeps you logged in and maintains your preferences. | Until you log out or your session expires (usually a few hours). |
| CSRF/security token | Helps protect your account against cross‑site request forgery attacks. | End of session |

## 2. Optional tracking

Wuwu Advisor does not currently use third‑party analytics or advertising cookies.  Our **first‑party instrumentation** records basic, pseudonymised usage events (e.g., which features are used) to improve the product.  These events are stored on our servers and not shared with third parties.  We will introduce a cookie banner and preference centre if we add optional cookies or external analytics in the future.

## 3. Your choices

Because no optional cookies are currently set, there is no cookie preference centre at this time.  Should we implement analytics or marketing cookies, you will be offered clear options to accept or reject them.  You can also adjust your browser settings to block or delete cookies, although this may affect functionality.
