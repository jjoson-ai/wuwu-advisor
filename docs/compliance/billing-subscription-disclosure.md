# Pro Subscription & Billing Disclosure

**Last updated:** 18 April 2026

This disclosure explains how billing and subscriptions work for Wuwu Advisor's **Pro** plan. It is based on the current implementation of our payment flow.

## 1. Pro subscription access

- Wuwu Advisor offers a free tier and an optional **Pro** tier. When you choose to subscribe, you will be directed to a **Stripe** checkout page. Stripe collects your payment details and processes the transaction on our behalf.
- After a successful payment, Stripe sends a webhook to our servers. Once we receive and verify this notification, your account is upgraded to Pro and additional features are unlocked. In rare circumstances, activation may be delayed by a few minutes. If your Pro access does not appear, please contact support with your payment confirmation.

## 2. Free trial

- **Annual Pro subscriptions include a 7-day free trial.** You will not be charged during the trial period.
- If you cancel before the trial ends you will not be charged at all.
- If you do not cancel, your subscription begins automatically at the end of the trial and you are charged the annual price.
- **Monthly subscriptions do not include a free trial** and are charged immediately on signup.
- You must be 18 or older to subscribe.

## 3. Billing & renewal

- **Billing period:** Pro subscriptions are available on a monthly or annual basis. The billing frequency is shown during checkout.
- **Auto-renewal:** Unless you cancel, your subscription automatically renews at the end of each billing cycle using the payment method on file.
- **Price changes:** If we change the subscription price, we will notify you in advance and give you the opportunity to cancel before the new price applies.

## 4. Cancellation & refunds

- **How to cancel:** You can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings on the website or app. Alternatively, contact our support team.
- **Effective date:** Cancellation becomes effective at the end of your current billing period. You will continue to have Pro access until that date.
- **Refunds:** We do not routinely offer prorated refunds for unused time. However, we will consider refund requests in cases of technical issues or accidental charges. Please contact support@wuwu-advisor.com within 14 days of the charge.
- **Failed payments:** If your payment fails, Stripe will attempt to collect again. If payment ultimately fails, your account will revert to the free tier and Pro features will be disabled.

## 5. Payment information & security

- Your payment details are handled by Stripe. We do not store or have access to your full card number. Stripe uses industry-standard encryption and security measures.
- We receive and store limited transaction metadata (such as subscription status and payment amount) to maintain your subscription record and comply with tax obligations.

## 6. Account deletion

Account deletion is immediate via the **Delete my account** button in Settings. Deletion removes all personal data and terminates any active subscription. Some billing records (e.g. invoices) may be retained to meet legal and tax obligations.

## 7. Support

For billing or subscription questions, please contact **support@wuwu-advisor.com**. Include your email address and subscription reference so we can locate your transaction.
