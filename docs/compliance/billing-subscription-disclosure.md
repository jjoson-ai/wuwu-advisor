# Pro Subscription & Billing Disclosure

**Last updated:** 22 March 2026

This disclosure explains how billing and subscriptions work for Wuwu Advisor’s **Pro** plan.  It is based on the current implementation of our payment flow.

## 1. Pro subscription access

- Wuwu Advisor offers a free tier and an optional **Pro** tier.  When you choose to subscribe, you will be directed to a **Stripe** checkout page.  Stripe collects your payment details and processes the transaction on our behalf.
- After a successful payment, Stripe sends a webhook to our servers.  Once we receive and verify this notification, your account is upgraded to Pro and additional features are unlocked.  We are currently hardening webhook reliability; in rare circumstances, activation may be delayed.  If your Pro access does not appear within a few minutes, please contact support with your payment confirmation.

## 2. Billing & renewal

- **Billing period:** Pro subscriptions are offered on a monthly or annual basis.  The billing frequency is indicated during checkout.
- **Auto‑renewal:** Unless you cancel, your subscription automatically renews at the end of each billing cycle using the payment method on file.
- **Price changes:** If we change the subscription price, we will notify you in advance and give you the opportunity to cancel before the new price applies.

## 3. Cancellation & refunds

- **How to cancel:** You can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings on the website/app.  Alternatively, you may contact our support team.
- **Effective date:** Cancellation becomes effective at the end of your current billing period.  You will continue to have Pro access until that date.
- **Refunds:** We do not routinely offer prorated refunds for unused time.  However, we will consider refund requests in cases of technical issues or accidental charges.  Please reach out to support@wuwu‑advisor.com within 14 days of the charge for assistance.
- **Failed payments:** If your payment fails, Stripe will attempt to collect again.  If payment ultimately fails, your account will revert to the free tier and Pro features will be disabled.

## 4. Payment information & security

- Your payment details are handled by Stripe.  We do not store or have access to your full card number.  Stripe uses industry‑standard encryption and security measures.
- We receive and store limited transaction metadata (such as the last four digits of your card, subscription status and payment amount) to maintain your subscription record and comply with tax obligations.

## 5. Support

For billing or subscription questions, please contact **support@wuwu‑advisor.com**.  When contacting us, include your email address and subscription reference so we can locate your transaction.  Because our deletion process is partially manual, please allow up to a few days for full cancellation or data removal to be reflected.
