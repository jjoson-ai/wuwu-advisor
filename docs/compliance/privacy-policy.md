Wuwu Advisor is an AI‑powered astrology and decision‑support app available via web and mobile.  We provide a free tier and an optional **Pro** subscription. ## 3. How we process data

- **LLM processing:** When you ask a question or we generate a forecast, your prompt and relevant context are sent to third‑party LLM providers (e.g., OpenAI or Anthropic) to produce an answer.  We pseudonymise the data before transfer (e.g., replacing your email with an internal ID) and prohibit model training on your content via contractual terms.  These providers act as our processors.
- **Hosting & authentication:** We store data on cloud servers in the **European Union** (e.g., Ireland).  Some support operations or backups may run outside the EEA.  Passwords are hashed; data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
- **Payments:** We use **Stripe** to handle payment processing.  Stripe collects your billing information and processes payments.  We receive only the necessary transaction metadata (e.g., subscription status, price, date).  Card details are not stored on our servers.
- **First‑party analytics:** We collect minimal usage metrics via our own instrumentation.  We do not currently use external analytics providers or third‑party marketing cookies.  If we introduce optional tracking, we will ask for consent via a banner or settings.

## 4. Transfers outside the EEA

When we transfer personal data of EU users to countries outside the European Economic Area (EEA), we implement safeguards.  Transfers to our LLM providers and Stripe (both based in the US) are governed by the **European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses**【250188135282283†L191-L233】 and additional contractual commitments to ensure appropriate protection.  We pseudonymise data wherever possible.  You can obtain a copy of these safeguards by contacting us.

## 5. How long we keep your data

We retain data only for as long as necessary for the purposes stated above.  See the table in Section 2 for typical retention periods.  Backups are kept for up to 90 days and then purged, except where legal obligations require longer retention (e.g., invoices).

## 6. Your rights

If you are in the EU or another jurisdiction that grants data subject rights, you have the right to:

- **Be informed** about how your data are used.
- **Access** your personal data and receive a copy【594987952245840†L59-L68】.
- **Rectify** inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erase** data (“right to be forgotten”).  Note: deletion currently involves a manual process; we will confirm via email when your data have been removed.  We may retain certain data (e.g., invoices) to meet legal obligations.
- **Restrict** or **object** to certain processing.
- **Port your data** to another service.
- **Not be subject to solely automated decisions** that produce legal or similarly significant effects【594987952245840†L59-L68】.  Wuwu Advisor provides reflective guidance and does not make decisions on your behalf.

To exercise any of these rights, please use the in‑app export or delete function (available in settings) or contact us at the email below.  We will respond within one month【594987952245840†L93-L100】.  For deletion requests, because our system does not yet have an automated deletion pipeline, a privacy team member will process your request manually.

## 7. Security

We implement technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encryption, pseudonymisation, role‑based access controls and regular security assessments.  We limit employee access to personal data and monitor systems for unauthorised activity.
