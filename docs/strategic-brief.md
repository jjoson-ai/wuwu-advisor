# Wuwu Advisor Strategic Brief

## Purpose
Wuwu Advisor helps people make clearer decisions and navigate timing better through a premium AI advisor that feels grounded, personal, and worth returning to daily.

## Target user
Primary user:
- emotionally intelligent
- spiritually curious
- wants pattern recognition and decision support without cringe, fluff, or generic horoscope content
- values continuity over one-off novelty

## Core problem
Most astrology products fall into one of three buckets:
- generic entertainment
- low-trust reader marketplaces
- isolated one-shot reports

They do not build a daily habit, and they do not compound into better future guidance.

## Core promise
- clearer decisions
- better timing
- premium, calm, non-deterministic guidance
- useful enough for everyday return
- deep enough for higher-intent moments

## Product surfaces and jobs
- **Today** — 24-hour operating brief
- **Blueprint** — stable identity and decision-style reference
- **Forecast** — medium-horizon planning layer
- **Ask** — direct decision/timing guidance for active questions

## Positioning
Wuwu Advisor is:
- not a horoscope feed
- not a psychic marketplace
- not generic AI coaching

It wins on:
- structured timing guidance
- continuity across surfaces
- premium trust
- the ability for premium interactions to improve future output quality over time

## Go-to-market
- launch web + mobile
- use free as the habit layer
- convert into Pro through Today, Forecast, Blueprint, and Ask
- favor web checkout and annual plans where practical because web economics are stronger than mobile

## Revenue direction
### Phase 1
- Free + Pro

### Long-term target
- Free + subscription + credits

Principle:
- subscription monetizes continuity and fuller premium access
- credits later monetize urgency, timing precision, and high-compute premium sessions

## North-star principle
Habit free; monetize urgency and compounding value.

Premium sessions should never end as dead-end artifacts. They should write structured context back into the product so future Today, Forecast, and Ask outputs improve.

## What to optimize at launch
1. free-to-paid conversion by surface
2. paid retention
3. Ask usage intensity
4. gross margin by tier / surface / platform
5. web checkout share
6. compute discipline

## What Wuwu Advisor is not
- not therapy
- not financial or medical advice
- not a generic astrology content farm
- not a one-shot report business
- not a pure entertainment meme app
- not a pre-launch monetization science project

## Operating rules
- launch with Free + Pro now
- do not delay launch for credits
- keep launch scope tight
- no major pre-launch rewrite
- sharpen free; make paid meaningfully deeper
- reflective, not deterministic
- premium, not mystical fluff
- credits later should start with premium Ask, timing reads, and relationship/career deep dives
- default to app-locale-based language later, then add user preference override after launch
